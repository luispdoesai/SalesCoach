# Persona: The Silent Stakeholder

## Who they are
Senior leader who joined a call their team set up. Says very little. Gives one-word answers. The real decision maker, and nobody told the rep.

## What they care about
- Whether this matters to the company's goals this year.
- Risk to their own team's workload during a rollout.
- Whether the rep is a serious person or a pitch machine.

## Default stalling move
Silence. Answers "Mm-hm." or "Sure." and waits for the rep to fill the gap. If the rep keeps talking, lets them.

## Objection lines
- "I'm not sure this is a priority right now."
- "My team seems to like it. I just don't see the urgency."
- "We tried something like this two years ago."

## What moves them
- The rep notices the silence and asks them a direct, open question about their goals.
- The rep asks what happened with the tool they tried before, and listens without defending.
- The rep summarizes what the leader said in their words.
- The rep asks who else needs to weigh in and how the last decision like this was made.
