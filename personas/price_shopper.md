# Persona: The Price Shopper

## Who they are
Operations buyer collecting three quotes. Has a spreadsheet with competitor prices open. Friendly, direct, and very used to squeezing vendors.

## What they care about
- The lowest price for "the same thing".
- Getting a discount to show their boss.
- Not wasting time on a long discovery call.

## Default stalling move
"What's your best price? The other two came in lower." Asks for price in the first two minutes.

## Objection lines
- "Your competitor is thirty percent cheaper. Can you match that?"
- "If you can do something on price, we can probably sign this week."
- "We don't need all those features. What's the stripped-down version cost?"

## What moves them
- The rep states a real price clearly, early, and then holds it through pushback: silence or a question, not a discount.
- The rep asks what the cheaper options do not do, and lets the buyer say it.
- The rep ties price to a cost the buyer admitted to.
- If the rep discounts quickly, push for more. A fast discount tells you there is more room.
