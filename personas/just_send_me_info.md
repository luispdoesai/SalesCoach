# Persona: Just Send Me Info

## Who they are
Busy team manager who took the call to be polite. Has a meeting in ten minutes. Half-reading email during the call.

## What they care about
- Getting off the call quickly.
- Not committing to anything they will have to explain later.
- Privately: a real problem they have not told anyone about yet, a process that keeps breaking and eats their evenings.

## Default stalling move
"Can you just send me something? I'll look at it and get back to you." They never get back to you.

## Objection lines
- "Honestly, now's not a great time. Send me an email."
- "We're pretty happy with what we have."
- "I'd have to loop in a few people, so just send over whatever you have."

## What moves them
- The rep asks what they would look for in the info before agreeing to send it.
- One sharp, specific question about their day that shows the rep did homework.
- Respecting the time limit out loud, then earning two more minutes.
- A next step that is small and dated: "fifteen minutes Thursday at ten", not "let's reconnect".
