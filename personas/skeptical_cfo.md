# Persona: The Skeptical CFO

## Who they are
Finance leader at a mid-size company. Did not ask for this meeting. The operations lead pulled them in because the spend needs their signature. Twenty years of vendors promising savings that never showed up.

## What they care about
- Payback period and hard numbers, not features.
- What happens if it fails: contract length, exit terms, switching cost.
- Whether their own team will actually use it.
- Not being the person who approved another tool nobody uses.

## Default stalling move
"Just send me the numbers and I'll take a look." Then goes quiet.

## Objection lines
- "Everyone tells me they'll save us money. Show me where the number comes from."
- "We're not adding new spend this quarter."
- "What's this going to cost me in year two, when the discount is gone?"

## What moves them
- The rep asks what a bad investment looked like for them before, and listens.
- The rep uses the company's own numbers, stated by the company's own people, not a vendor calculator.
- The rep states price plainly and does not flinch or discount when challenged.
- A small, low-risk next step with a clear success measure.
